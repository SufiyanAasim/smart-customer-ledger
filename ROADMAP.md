# Product Roadmap

## Releases Architecture

- **v1.0.0 — Polaris — Index**: Core RDBMS architecture, CRUD, Auth, Views & Triggers.
- **v2.0.0 — Zenith — Balance**: ACID Financial settlement workflows & Account reconciliation.
- **v3.0.0 — Pulsar — Snapshot**: Database backup/restore & CSV/JSON data import/export.
- **v4.0.0 — Nebula — Chronicle**: Data dictionary generation & Mermaid ERD visualizations.
- **v5.0.0 — Nova — Replica**: Read/Write replica separation & Health monitoring.
- **v6.0.0 — Orion — Shard**: Logical database sharding & Cross-shard analytics.
- **v7.0.0 — Atlas — Capital**: Logistic Regression Risk Model & RFM Customer Segmentation.
